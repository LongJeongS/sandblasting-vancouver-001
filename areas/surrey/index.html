About the Area

About Surrey

Surrey is one of the largest, fastest-growing parts of Metro Vancouver, and our rig ranges across all of it — the industrial and warehouse pockets of Newton and Cloverdale, plus the busy commercial corridors of Guildford and Fleetwood. We bring the equipment to your shop or yard.

A lot of Surrey is working commercial and light-industrial space: auto shops, fabricators, and warehouses. That means roll-up doors, steel frames, and fleet equipment that need to be blasted down to clean metal before a coating or repaint, and we turn that around quickly.

The newer Cloverdale and Clayton developments bring fresh steel and concrete that need proper surface prep, while the older Newton industrial stock often comes with years of layered paint and rust to cut through.

With the damp climate working against bare steel, we can prime right after blasting so it doesn't flash-rust overnight. Give us the surface and your address and we'll come out for a written estimate.
